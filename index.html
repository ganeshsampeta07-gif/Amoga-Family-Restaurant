Welcome to Amoga Family Restaurant

Amoga Family Restaurant is one of Khammam's most loved dining spots, known for its delicious food, welcoming service, and cozy family-friendly atmosphere.

Whether you're dining with family, meeting friends, or enjoying a relaxed dinner, our restaurant offers a comfortable space where great food and great memories come together.

Our menu features a wide variety of flavorful dishes prepared with care and served in generous portions at affordable prices.
